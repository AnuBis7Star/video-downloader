Video Downloader
================

Downloads YouTube videos and playlists to a folder on your Mac. Paste links, pick a quality,
press Download. It keeps its download engine (yt-dlp) up to date on its own.

Needs: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 12 or later.


Install
-------
1. Drag "Video Downloader.app" into your Applications folder.
2. Open it once with these steps, because it isn't from the App Store or a registered
   Apple developer, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time:

   a. Double-click Video Downloader. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click "Done"
      (or "OK"). Don't move it to the Bin.
   b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about
      "Video Downloader", and click "Open Anyway". Enter your password if asked.
   c. Click "Open Anyway" once more in the dialog that appears.

   After that it opens normally, like any other app.


Using it
--------
- Paste one or more YouTube links (videos or playlists) into the big box.
- Choose a quality: Best, 1080p, 720p, 480p, or Audio only (mp3).
- Press Download. The first time, it asks which folder to save to. Tick "Always here"
  to skip that question next time.
- The x on a row skips that video. Cancel stops everything and deletes what that
  download had saved so far.


Please use it for videos you have the right to download.
